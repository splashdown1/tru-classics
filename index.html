Recommended workflow

1. Prefer Standard Ebooks when available (cleanest modern public-domain formatting).
2. Otherwise use Project Gutenberg HTML or plain text.
3. Download the HTML / EPUB / TXT.
4. Convert or wrap into a single-file TRU-style reader (or keep as a modular data pack + thin shell).
5. Add a short provenance card: title, author, translator, source URL, date fetched, license note.
6. Place under a new lane such as tru-classics or as cards under the existing shelf.

The goal is the same as every other TRU lane: one place a person can open with no internet and still have the source.
